# Accessibility Audit
## Problem: Background and Foreground Colors do not have Sufficient Contrast Ratio
**Explanation:** The background 2 color and text highlight color don't have enough contrast between them. This appears on the navigation buttons as well as in project cards. This was the only accessibility problem found with the site.

**Solution:** The background 2 color was changed from #4B5043 to #333333 to increase the contrast ratio to 7.01.
