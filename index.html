About

Hello! I am an undergraduate student at Michigan State University and I double-major in both Information Science and Games and Interactive Media. I focus my information science major on learning human-centered design, and I use that in user interface and gameplay system designs when making video games.

Video games have been a hobby of mine from a young age, and it has since become a passion of mine to break down and analyze gameplay systems to understand how they work and how I think they could be improved. I also love playing with friends and using my knowledge to help others succeed. In the future, I hope to help build these kinds of games where people can use their understanding of the world to help one another overcome great challenges.
